M00 Answer Key and Gap Sprints

Open this only after you have submitted the diagnostic and I have scored it. Reading answers first ruins the measurement and the retake. Below: how to score, the full key with rubrics, then six timeboxed sprints that close the gaps in the order the course needs them.

Rule. The retest uses new questions on the same skills, so memorizing this key does not help. Understanding does.

1. Score yourself

Total is 56 points. Enter the points you earned in each section (rubrics are in section 2). Colors decide which sprints you run.

Green is 90% or more (mastered). Amber is 70 to 89% (one review pass). Red is below 70% (run the full sprint, then retest).

2. Answer key

Short answers: award the points in the rubric, partial credit allowed. Numbers: full credit within 3% of the key, half credit for the right method with an arithmetic slip.

3. Gap sprints

Each sprint is 60 to 90 minutes and follows the same loop: predict first, learn the minimum, build a small thing, then prove it by explaining it. Sprints marked red or amber by your score float to the top of your plan; green ones become a 10-minute flashcard review.

4. Seven-day schedule

Techniques used, and why

  • Prediction before reveal. Every sprint starts by writing your number, then running code. Wrong predictions are where the learning is.
  • Retrieval over rereading. Flashcards are added to the spaced-repetition deck (tools/learn.py card add), reviewed daily.
  • Build to learn. Small scripts you write yourself, saved as evidence and later reused in A3 and A4.
  • Socratic AI tutoring. Ask me for a hint, not the answer. Hints escalate from a question to a nudge to a worked example of a different problem.
  • Interleaving. From day 3, each session opens with two questions from earlier sprints.
  • Numbers as a habit. Course assignments are graded on measured tokens, cost and latency, so every sprint ends with a quantity you computed or measured.
